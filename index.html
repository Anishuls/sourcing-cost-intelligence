Sourcing Cost Intelligence

Drop in a PO Data export: elastic, hook & eye, fabric or a full list. It compares one supplier's prices with every other supplier, by type and size. The file is read on this computer and is not uploaded anywhere.

What you get

  1. How much you bought from the supplier you pick (Suntak by default), and how much more you paid than Elastique or Takefast for the same type and width.
  2. A table by type: average price, extra paid and saved.
  3. Every article of that supplier, with the matching Elastique, Takefast and other suppliers' prices, cheapest first.

Same article used by several buyers

One row per supplier, article and width. Shows how many buyers it was bought for and the average price each buyer paid. A price difference is flagged when the highest buyer price is more than 0.5% above the lowest. Extra paid = what was paid above the lowest buyer price for the same article. Follows the type filter; prices can differ because of colour or the PO date.
